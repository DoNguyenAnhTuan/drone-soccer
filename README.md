# 🛸 Drone Soccer @ EIU FabLab

This repository contains all materials used in organizing and running the **Drone Soccer** competition at **Eastern International University (EIU)**. It includes simulation setups, training slides, field diagrams, and tools for real-time scoring and coordination.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Promote STEM learning through engaging drone competitions.
- Provide hands-on training on drone control, team strategy, and safety.
- Introduce students to real-world applications of robotics and autonomous flight.

---

## 📁 Contents

- `field/`: CAD files and field diagrams for building the arena.
- `slides/`: PDF/PPT training materials for rules, drone control, and coding basics.
- `rules/`: Competition rulebooks and safety guidelines.
- `scoreboard/`: A simple scoreboard system (web-based).
- `sim/`: (optional) simulation tools or playground.

---

## 🛠 Technologies & Tools

- Drone: Balkan SkyKick2 Starter Kit / Tello (optional)
- Language: Python, Scratch, Blockly (depending on team level)
- Software: QGroundControl / Drone blocks / Custom scoreboard

---

## 📸 Highlights

- Indoor drone arena setup at EIU FabLab 🏟️
- Scoreboard with live updates
- Simple drone controller for training beginners
- Used in summer STEM camps and university-level competitions
